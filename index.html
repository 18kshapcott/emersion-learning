🗣 Case Parameters to Obtain Via Interview

Include key subjective information that would be obtained during a patient interview. In the first column, write the question or word that a student would need to ask to trigger the patient's response in the second column.

✅ Examples:

Adherence:
Misses doses 2–3 times per week due to forgetfulness

Symptoms:
Reports increased fatigue and nocturia

Lifestyle:
Diet high in processed foods, exercises rarely

Barriers:
States cost is preventing medication adherence

• Focus on clinically relevant details
• Write in short, clear statements
• Avoid full paragraphs

🧪 Lab Entry Format

Enter lab values with current value first, followed by previous values.

✅ Example:
8.2% | 8.7% (3 months ago), 9.1% (6 months ago)

• Use | to separate current from past
• Include timeframes when possible
• Keep units consistent (%, mg/dL, etc.)

💡 Writing Effective Hints

Hints should guide student thinking, not give away answers.

🔹 Hint 1 — Broad Direction
Encourage general clinical thinking

🔹 Hint 2 — Focus Attention
Direct toward key labs or findings

🔹 Hint 3 — Near Answer
Guide toward diagnosis or plan

🔹 Hint 4+ (Optional)
Reinforce guidelines or decision points

✅ Tips:
• Build each hint step-by-step
• Be clear and specific
• Think like a preceptor guiding a learner

⚠️ Avoid:
• Giving the answer too early
• Repeating the same idea
• Vague hints like “look carefully”
